FAQ

Straight answers.

What is GrokSync?

GrokSync is a self-hosted private board so your PC, laptop, phone, and agents share one continue packet. Sit down anywhere and already know what you were doing on the others.

Is there a hosted GrokSync account?

No. You clone the MIT repo and host the site yourself. There is no public hosted account.

What is a continue packet?

A short, redacted snapshot of the last project, a one-line note, and optional next step. It travels with you. Local claim checks stay on the machine you sat down at.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. GrokSync is a website you host. Open your board URL in any browser on phone, laptop, or desktop.

Where does my data live?

On the host you deploy. Password gate. Optional encrypted notes. Nothing here is a vendor cloud that reads your disk.

What license is it under?

MIT. Fonts under public/fonts/fontshare keep their ITF Free Font License files.
